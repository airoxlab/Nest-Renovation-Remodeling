Services

Everything inside the four walls.

Kitchen remodels

Layout changes, cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring, lighting and plumbing fixtures, managed as one project.

Bathroom remodels

Flooring & tile

Room additions & repairs

Kitchens & bathrooms

The two rooms that change how a house feels.

Most of what we do starts here. Both are plumbing, electrical, tile and cabinetry in a small space, which is exactly where experience shows.

A kitchen remodel includes

  • Demo and haul-away
  • Layout and electrical updates
  • Cabinets and hardware
  • Countertops and backsplash
  • Sink, faucet and appliance hookups
  • Flooring, paint and lighting

Typical timeline two to four weeks depending on scope and material lead times.

A bathroom remodel includes

  • Demo and haul-away
  • Waterproofing and shower pan
  • Tile walls and floors
  • Vanity, toilet and fixtures
  • Exhaust fan and lighting
  • Paint and finish trim

Typical timeline one to two weeks for a standard hall bath.

How it goes

Four steps, no surprises.

Walk-through

Build
